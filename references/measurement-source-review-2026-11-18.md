# Measurement program research and sourcing — 18 November 2026

These are **public reference materials** guiding a **local proposed MSSP program**. They do **not** specify this project's unique metric catalog, its phases, thresholds, dashboard hierarchy or customer contracts.

| Source | What we apply | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|

| [NIST SP 800-55 Vol. 2 — Developing a Measurement Program](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/55/v2/final) | Measurement program governance, maintenance and communication | No particular software or numeric risk grade |

## Design conclusions from the sources

1. **Ask what decision a metric supports** before choosing data and graphics.
2. **Keep risk judgment distinct from measurement**; the customer and authorized risk authority determine mission consequence and treatment.
3. **Separate the MSSP's performance from the customer's protection** and from the quality/completeness of available evidence.
4. **Do not average vendor scores.** Define shared outcomes and reproducible evidence requirements instead.
5. **Start honestly with inherited logs**. Do not calculate coverage where expected scope or denominators are unknown.
6. **Make provenance and limitations visible at every audience level**; otherwise attractive dashboards will overstate assurance.
7. **Use one master metric register** to avoid four overlapping programs; specialized domains use the same specification rules.

## What still needs local decisions

Risk appetite/thresholds, accepted service commitments, customer-criticality designations, which evidence is authoritative, validated attribution, measure ownership, reporting cadence, and final approvals. Access controls, technical integrations and software architecture are outside the program scope and are deliberately not resolved here.
