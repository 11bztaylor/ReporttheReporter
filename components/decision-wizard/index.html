WHAT THIS DOES

Shows the full handoff

Prompts technical and program teams for the minimum defensible information, checks coverage and context, identifies the appropriate escalation path, and creates a briefing.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

It cannot certify security

There are no source integrations, automatic risk scores, or approvals. Conclusions require human validation and organizational policy.
