**Gate:** If a statement needs a missing eligible population or unverified business impact, withhold the *unsupported claim*. You may still report the legitimate **knowledge gap and engagement step**.

## B. Executive — concise decision brief

> **What matters:** [condition or important unknown, with relevant scope and date].  
> **Why leadership should care:** [plausible consequence or enabling decision; mark impact unverified when appropriate].  
> **How sure are we:** [observation and validation basis, limitations].  
> **Decision/request:** [who should authorize what; provider/customer/authorized risk authority].  
> **Next check:** [what changes would justify concluding progress].

## C. Management — action-oriented interpretation

> **Measurement:** [ID, definition/version, stage, applicability].  
> **Why this is showing up:** [trend, evidence, alternate causes, validity].  
> **Ownership:** [provider / customer / shared / unresolved with explanation].  
> **Next work:** [specific validation/remediation/customer question, proposed owner].  
> **Reassessment:** [review date and criterion for updating executives].

## D. Technical — reproducible validation note

> **Observed:** [what types of evidence, date/time and source context].  
> **Quality:** [source freshness, field meaning, duplication, time skew, valid population basis].  
> **Not established:** [source/customer identity/eligible scope/criticality/actual control outcome].  
> **Test to perform:** [reproducible validation question; example evidence types].  
> **Promotion gate:** [exact missing evidence/approval necessary for the next stronger claim].

## E. Illustrated dry run — *entirely hypothetical and without counts*

**Situation:** A new MSSP sees inherited security records suggestive of a customer's public-facing service, but the customer has not yet confirmed application ownership or subscribed monitoring scope.

| Audience | Wording |
|---|---|
| Executive | "We have observations associated with a possible public-facing service. Ownership, applicable protection scope and business importance are not yet confirmed. A customer review is needed before describing coverage or exposure." |
| Manager | "Record source attribution as provisional. Request validation of the public service owner and expected scope. Separately check whether provider evidence quality is sufficient for later controls testing." |
| Technical | "Retain dated representative source references, explain attribution heuristics, check duplicate identities and clarify which application/security-control claims are not established by these records." |

**After customer engagement:** The same item may become a scoped N01/N03/N05 measurement **only if** attribution, cohort and applicable controls are validated. If the customer determines it is not in scope, mark **not applicable with rationale**, not as a customer failure.

## F. Review checklist

- [ ] No invented device totals, risk probability, cost loss, "0% readiness", or coverage claim.
- [ ] A customer assertion is not mislabeled as provider verification.
- [ ] Adoption horizon is not confused with performance.
- [ ] Provider fault is not silently attributed to customer.
- [ ] Decision authority and follow-up are specified, or explicitly unresolved.
- [ ] The executive, management and technical versions reflect the **same underlying claim**.
