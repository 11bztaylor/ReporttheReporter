## Before publication

- [ ] The question could actually lead to an authorized action or decision.
- [ ] Customer/capability scope and applicability have been established as needed.
- [ ] Denominator (if a ratio) is **eligible, defensible and time-qualified**; never assume observed-only inventory is all there is.
- [ ] Evidence supports the exact strength of the statement (not merely a vendor's score).
- [ ] Any limitations, stale data, unassessed assets and contradictory sources are visible.
- [ ] Provider service shortcomings and customer-owned conditions are not confused.
- [ ] The same finding can be communicated faithfully in three audience levels.
- [ ] A decision/action owner and outcome check exist if escalation is warranted.

**If validation is missing:** publish the knowledge gap or validation request, not a misleading security percentage. All examples in this public repository are illustrative and must not contain sensitive customer findings.
